The NFL Predictive Pricing Model estimates a fair winning margin and game total for each matchup. This page compares those model prices with the captured market consensus to show where the model and market differ.

Sportsbook Edge and Total Edge are differences in points between model pricing and market pricing. They are not guarantees of profit or winning wagers.

How to Read This Page

Model Fair Line
The point spread implied by the model's predicted scoring margin.
Market Consensus
The certified consensus spread reconstructed from eligible market observations available at the forecast horizon.
Sportsbook Edge (ATS)
The point difference between the model's fair margin and the market-implied margin.
Model Total
The model's predicted combined final score.
Total Edge
The difference between the model total and market total.
Winner View
Whether the model and market favor the same team outright.

Lines move. Compare the model with the price currently available at your sportsbook before making a decision.

Model Methodology & Review

The model's construction, data sources, validation record and known limitations are documented in the public review package: Model Methodology & Review.

Forecasts are created before results are known and archived by forecast horizon for prospective evaluation.

Model prices are estimates. Nothing on this page is a demonstration of sportsbook profitability, and nothing here is betting advice.

All kickoff, model and market times are shown in United States Eastern Time (America/New_York).
