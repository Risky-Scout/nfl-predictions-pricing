# Week 1 Shadow Reliability 2026 — Pre-Registration

**Status:** PRE-REGISTERED. Written and committed **before** any outcome metric
(log loss, Brier, ECE, calibration, bootstrap, reliability status) is computed.
Nothing below is edited after results are seen; any post-hoc correction is a
dated amendment at the bottom that fixes an *implementation defect* without
changing a substantive decision rule.

This is an **evaluation-only** stage (Stage 3). It measures the Week 1 historical
statistical reliability of the frozen shadow model. It does **not** promote the
shadow, does **not** establish betting edge, and changes **no** production
behavior. Production remains:

```
production_probability = market_probability
production_source      = MARKET_BASELINE
```

Stage 3 may PASS even when the statistical conclusion is
`INCONCLUSIVE_SMALL_SAMPLE` or `CALIBRATION_CONCERN`.

## 1. Terminology (frozen)

The evaluated model is named **`market_augmented_epa_rest_shadow`** throughout.

Its 19 frozen features include `home_spread` and `total_line`; it is therefore
**not independent of the betting market**. The existing output column
`fundamental_probability` is retained for backward compatibility, but:

> **`fundamental_probability` is generated by the market-augmented EPA/rest shadow
> model and is not independent of the market.** It is not a pure football-only
> probability.

Three probabilities are never conflated:
- `market_probability` — de-vigged market baseline (see §7).
- `shadow_probability` (a.k.a. `fundamental_probability`) — the market-augmented
  EPA/rest shadow model output.
- `production_probability` — the governed market baseline (unchanged).

## 4. Feature & outcome definitions (frozen)

Features are the 19 `FROZEN_FEATURES` built by
`build_augmented_feature_matrix` (leakage-safe shift-then-roll; each metric
shifted one game before any rolling/expanding calc). Season-to-date (`season_mean`)
EPA features reset per season and are therefore unavailable (NaN → imputed) in
Week 1; `last4_mean` EPA features and rest features carry from prior seasons.

Outcomes (per `_outcome_columns`), computed from final scores:
- `home_margin = home_score − away_score`; `total_points = home_score + away_score`.
- **Moneyline:** home win = `home_margin > 0`. A **tie** (`home_margin == 0`) is
  neither a home nor away win: ties are **excluded** from moneyline scoring and
  reported separately. Never coded as an arbitrary win.
- **Spread (home cover at quoted point `s = home_spread`):** cover =
  `home_margin + s > 0`. **Push** at integer `s` when `home_margin + s == 0`.
  Pushes are **excluded** from graded cover scoring and reported separately.
- **Total (over at quoted point `t = total_line`):** over = `total_points > t`.
  **Push** at integer `t` when `total_points == t`. Pushes excluded, reported
  separately.

The quoted contract point is exactly the `home_spread` / `total_line` the shadow
consumed as a feature. Shadow no-push/no-tie probabilities
(`home_win_probability_no_tie`, `home_cover_probability_no_push`,
`over_probability_no_push`) are scored against the non-push / non-tie outcomes.

## 5. Evaluation populations

**A. LOCKED 2025 WEEK 1 HOLDOUT (confirmation set).** The existing production
shadow artifact (trained 2020–2023, calibrated 2024, `holdout_excluded=2025`)
predicts 2025 Week 1. Admissible only because, by artifact construction, 2025
outcomes entered no fitting, calibration, feature selection, or hyperparameter
selection, and the artifact predates this evaluation. One Week 1 (16 games) is
too small to alone establish calibration.

**B. POOLED ROLLING-ORIGIN WEEK 1 OOF (primary).** Genuine out-of-fold Week 1
predictions under the frozen spec. Folds:

| Test season Y | Train | Calibrate | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | seasons ≤ 2020 | 2021 | Week 1 of 2022 |
| 2023 | seasons ≤ 2021 | 2022 | Week 1 of 2023 |
| 2024 | seasons ≤ 2022 | 2023 | Week 1 of 2024 |
| 2025 | seasons ≤ 2023 | 2024 | Week 1 of 2025 |

For test season Y: no Y outcomes in training; no Y rows fit imputation,
preprocessing, or calibration; no Y outcomes select anything. A fold that cannot
be formed is excluded with a documented reason. (Fold 2025 shares the production
artifact's train/calibrate windows and is expected to reproduce it — used as an
integrity cross-check.) Expected pooled Week 1 OOF n = 4 × 16 = **64** games.

**C. WEEKS 1–2 SENSITIVITY.** Same folds, pooled Weeks 1–2. Sample-size
sensitivity only; does not replace the Week 1 result. Expected n ≈ 4 × 32 = 128.

**D. LATER-WEEK CONTEXT.** Same folds, Weeks 3+ out-of-fold performance, reported
only as context for whether the Week 1 regime behaves differently. Not used to
tune Week 1.

## 7. Market matching (frozen)

**PRIMARY market baseline — `SCHEDULE_REFERENCE` (all seasons, exact contract).**
For every game, proportional de-vig (`devig_proportional`, the repository default
/ artifact-selected method) of the two-sided nflverse schedule-reference prices,
quoted at exactly the `home_spread_reference` / `total_line_reference` contract
the shadow consumed:
- Moneyline: de-vig(`home_moneyline_reference`, `away_moneyline_reference`) →
  `market P(home win | no tie)`.
- Spread: de-vig(`home_spread_price_reference`, `away_spread_price_reference`) at
  `home_spread_reference` → `market P(home cover | no push)`.
- Total: de-vig(`over_price_reference`, `under_price_reference`) at
  `total_line_reference` → `market P(over | no push)`.

Contract match is **exact by construction** (same game, market, side, point;
coherent two-way pair). Consensus is trivial (single reference source →
equal-mean of one). **Timing:** `line_reference_type = nflverse_schedule_reference`,
`line_timestamp_known = false`, no timestamp. This is labeled a
**schedule-reference closing-line proxy**. This is the market baseline for the
headline shadow-vs-market comparison, giving **identical paired games across all
folds including 2025**.

**SECONDARY market cross-check — real multi-book `CLOSING` (2022–2024 only).**
From the purchased odds: exclude in-play (`minutes_to_kickoff ≤ 0`, `is_live`),
select each game's closing snapshot (smallest positive `minutes_to_kickoff`),
take equal-mean (mean) cross-book de-vigged (`devig_probability`) consensus per
market. A row is included **only** when the consensus closing contract point
equals the shadow's reference contract point (spread/total); otherwise it is
excluded as `CONTRACT_MISMATCH`. Coverage losses and exclusion reasons are
reported. This is a robustness cross-check, not the primary comparison.

**Labels & prohibitions.** The analysis is labeled **closing-line historical
reliability**. Closing-line (and schedule-reference) evaluation does **not**
prove T-60 or T-10 reliability; those horizons are not fabricated. No
post-kickoff quote is used. No mixing of different spread points (e.g. −2.5 vs
−3) or different total points. Market-vs-shadow comparisons use only paired rows
whose probabilities refer to the identical contract.

## 8. Metrics (frozen)

Per market and population, on the graded (non-push / non-tie) rows:
- **log loss** (primary), **Brier score**, **equal-mass ECE**, **calibration
  intercept**, **calibration slope**, **AUC** (diagnostic only), prediction mean
  & sd, sample count, tie/push count, paired market coverage.
- Spread/total additionally report push count, observed push rate, and (where
  the frozen output supplies a coherent distribution) push handling. The frozen
  shadow supplies **binary** no-push cover / no-push over probabilities, so
  pushes are scored **separately**, never as win/loss/half.

**Calibration slope/intercept:** logistic regression of outcome on
`logit(clip(p, 1e-6, 1−1e-6))`; slope = coefficient, intercept = bias. Reported
`NOT_ESTIMABLE` when n < 30, a single outcome class is present, or the fit is
separated / non-finite.

**ECE (equal-mass, adaptive bins):**
`n_bins = max(3, min(10, floor(n / 12)))`, ≥ 12 observations per bin. ECE is
`NOT_ESTIMABLE` when n < 36 (cannot form 3 bins of ≥ 12).

Any metric not estimable from sample size or separation is reported
`NOT_ESTIMABLE`, never forced.

## 10. Bootstrap (frozen)

- **Replications:** 5,000. **Unit:** game. **Stratification:** season.
- **Seed:** `20260801` (`numpy.random.default_rng(20260801)`).
- **Paired** design: shadow and market log-loss / Brier differences use the
  **same** resampled game indices. 95% interval = percentile [2.5, 97.5].
- **Interpretation:** negative (shadow − market) favors shadow; positive favors
  market; an interval containing 0 is **statistically inconclusive**; superiority
  is never claimed from a point estimate alone.
- **Leave-one-season-out (LOSO)** sensitivity for the pooled Week 1 OOF
  population: recompute the pooled shadow−market log-loss difference with each
  one test season removed; report whether the sign/conclusion changes.

## 11. Reliability statuses (frozen decision rules)

Minimum sample for a **conclusive** per-market classification: **100 games**.
Non-inferiority margin (shadow vs market): **+0.02 log loss**. ECE practical
threshold **0.05**; slope practical range **0.75–1.25**; intercept practical
range **−0.10 to +0.10**.

Assign one status per market (evaluated on population B, pooled Week 1 OOF):

- **`WITHHOLD_SHADOW_OUTPUT`** if any: leakage detected; artifact inconsistency;
  nonfinite or out-of-[0,1] probabilities; nondeterministic predictions; gross
  scoring or contract mismatch; unrecoverable report inconsistency.
- **`INCONCLUSIVE_SMALL_SAMPLE`** if (no withhold condition and) pooled Week 1
  OOF n < 100; or required calibration diagnostics are `NOT_ESTIMABLE`; or
  uncertainty is too wide to determine practical reliability.
- **`CALIBRATION_CONCERN`** only when n ≥ 100 and one or more: ECE > 0.05; slope
  outside 0.75–1.25; intercept outside −0.10..+0.10; shadow materially worse than
  market with the paired 95% interval excluding the +0.02 margin; material
  cross-season instability.
- **`SUPPORTED_FOR_SHADOW_MONITORING`** only when all: n ≥ 100; no leakage /
  artifact defect; all probabilities finite & valid; ECE ≤ 0.05; slope
  0.75–1.25; intercept −0.10..+0.10; stable LOSO; shadow non-inferior to market
  within +0.02 log loss. **This status does not promote the shadow.** Production
  remains `MARKET_BASELINE`.

Because expected pooled Week 1 OOF n = 64 (< 100), the anticipated status is
`INCONCLUSIVE_SMALL_SAMPLE` for every market unless a `WITHHOLD` defect is
detected. This expectation is derived from sample **structure**, not outcomes.

**Production recommendation mapping (frozen):**
- any market `WITHHOLD_SHADOW_OUTPUT` → **`WITHHOLD_SHADOW_UNTIL_REPAIRED`**;
- else if any market `CALIBRATION_CONCERN` → **`RETAIN_MARKET_BASELINE`**;
- else (all `INCONCLUSIVE_SMALL_SAMPLE` and/or `SUPPORTED_FOR_SHADOW_MONITORING`,
  shadow leakage-safe/finite/deterministic) →
  **`RETAIN_MARKET_BASELINE_AND_MONITOR_SHADOW`**.

Stage 3 never recommends shadow promotion.
